# AE1 — cosmic-ray cross-section anomaly >1 TeV (deferred Tier B)

**Claim:** ~10^-6 enhancement in cosmic-ray spectrum at energies > 1 TeV.

**Data needed:** AMS-02 and CALET public spectra. Both publish summary
spectra in their PRL/PRD papers.

**Why deferred:** the predicted enhancement (10^-6) is ~3 orders of
magnitude below the statistical sensitivity of current public summaries
at TeV energies. The test is well-defined but data is insufficient.

**Promotion criterion:** AMS-02 / CALET decade-integrated spectra release
with ~10^-6 fractional sensitivity in the > 1 TeV bins.
