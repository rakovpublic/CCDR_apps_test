# FR10 — multi-machine confinement residuals (deferred Tier B)

**Claim:** Residuals from the IPB98(y,2) scaling correlate with M_KSS at
ITPA-DB scale.

**Data needed:** The full ITPA H-mode DB (>30 machines, ~5000 entries).
Public summaries exist, but the per-shot table is not on a public download.

**Promotion criterion:** Public release of the ITPA DB or a peer-reviewed
publication of per-machine residuals against an M_KSS-like variable.
