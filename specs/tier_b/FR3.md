# FR3 — ELM energy scaling (deferred Tier B)

**Claim:** ELM energy ~ pedestal pressure squared with a CCDR-specific
prefactor.

**Data needed:** JET / DIII-D ELM time series with synchronised pedestal
diagnostics. Available to ITPA participants under MoU; not on a public
download endpoint.

**Why deferred:** scraping per-shot ELM tables from JET-PPF or DIII-D
MDSplus archives requires user credentials. Public summary tables (e.g.
Federici et al. 2003) average over so many shots that the per-event scaling
is washed out.

**Promotion criterion:** A public release of per-ELM event tables with
synced pedestal n_e, T_e, and ELM energy fraction across at least two
machines.
